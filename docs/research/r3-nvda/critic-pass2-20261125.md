# R3 NVDA ledger: critic pass 2 (different model family), 2026-11-25

Research note for #1085. Docs only. **The frozen v2 files (`ledger-v2.jsonl`, `scenarios-v2.json`, `health-v2.json`, `synthesis-v2.md`) are not changed**; they are hash-pinned, so corrections are listed here as errata and would take effect only in a v3.

## What was done

`ledger-v2.jsonl` (455 records, sha256 `c593b397…`) was checked once by a critic of the same model family as its author (200 records: 176 confirmed, 20 corrected, 4 unverifiable; 255 records were not critic-checked). #1085 asks for an independent primary-source critic, so every record was run through a second pass by a different model family (Gemini 4 Neon, headless, read-only). For each record it checked the claim against the exact source document the record's `retrieved_sha256` covers: quote verbatim, value/units/currency, fiscal period, publication time, and classification (stage, kind, basis, conditions, circularity). It had to return a short verbatim `evidence` string per record.

Taking critic 1 for its 200 records and Neon for the other 255, all 455 have a critic verdict: 346 confirmed, 101 corrected, 8 unverifiable.

## How the second pass was re-checked

- Every `evidence` string (447 records with a source file) exists in the source text: 0 missing.
- Random samples of 25 Neon-confirmed records from each set (seed 1085): 50 of 50 agree.
- 255-record set: of 85 flags, 79 confirmed against the source text or the record's own quote, 1 rated lower in severity, 5 not re-derived.
- 200-record set: the 10 counted lines were checked one by one; the other flags were not re-derived (Neon's evidence string is present for each).
- Neon missed 4 problems in the 255 set that the recheck found (a currency on a percentage; three values that are not in the quoted passage but are elsewhere in the same document).

## What it means

- **No counted commitment value is disputed.** None of the 255 records is a counted line in the $542.4B stack. Of the 10 counted lines among the 200, Neon agrees with every value.
- **One counted label is disputed:** `nvda-10q-q2fy27-ai-cloud-agreements` ($36B). The ledger tags it "firm (maximum; decreases as capacity is used by third parties)". Neon would tag it contingent, citing the 10-Q's statement that the AI clouds "can unilaterally stop providing to us and sell to third-party customers". If relabelled, the firm subtotal would be $82.0B instead of $118.0B and the contingent subtotal $169.5B instead of $133.5B; the $542.4B total does not change, and the cap and buy-back tag are already disclosed in `synthesis-v2.md` §2.
- Most corrections are labels: in the 255 set 59 of the 81 touch classification (37 only classification); in the 200 set 66 of the 80 (52 only classification). Eight of the value flags are a currency set on a percentage or a gigawatt figure. Several period flags apply a different convention (the ledger often uses the filing's period, or a "reported 2026-11-17" pre-report marker, as `fiscal_period`).
- Neon applies a stricter standard than critic 1 (about 32% corrected against 10%); read the two as complementary, not as one rejecting the other. The counterparty-health grades in `health-v2.json` were not re-derived from these corrections.
- Where Neon disagrees with critic 1 on a record critic 1 had corrected (6 records), critic 1's corrections were applied before the freeze, so Neon is checking the corrected text.

## Limits

Neon is a model, not a human reviewer. Its verdicts are unverified except where the recheck above says otherwise. Secondary-only records (search-summary stubs, blocked sites) remain unverifiable.
