The diagnostic consumes explicitly provided canonical updates; no vendor/path access, fetch, registry, scoring or attempt to qualify live data. It is intentionally not invoked by any existing running service. The quote-mark gate accepts a single generation, uses the newest causal update for each supplied fixed leg, and refuses invalid or conflicting latest states rather than borrowing older quotes. It applies the historical revision 6 60-second/maximum-spread rule and tests DST instant comparison. A successful check reports FOUR_QUOTES_QUALIFIED and NOT_ESTABLISHED for execution; it cannot label MODELED_BBO_SAME_MARK without separately bound matching spot. ATM selection, spot/deadline policy, actual eligibility, contracts, fills and payoff are separate. Before any use beyond fixtures, the caller must freeze the immutable, complete input generation and mark, and authenticate and qualify update clocks, identities and sources. Differing prices at the same observation are rejected even with different arrival times because arrival is not an authoritative revision sequence; identical repeats retain the earliest eligible availability. Malformed/nonfinite or unsupported Decimal scales and magnitudes fail closed rather than permitting ambient arithmetic to alter the result. The diagnostic admits at most 18 Decimal coefficient digits, exponents −12 through 6, and an upper price bound of $1 million (input safety, not a strategy filter).

### Historical G5 context: Databento OPRA minute clock caveat
Databento's [CBBO schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas/bbo#consolidated-bbo-on-interval-cbbo) says a trade-only interval can carry the prior NBBO forward. Its minute `ts_recv` is the interval boundary; `ts_event` describes the last trade, not a book update. Neither proves per-leg quote age or when this observation reached GRID. A trade stamped 13:59:50 ET does not refresh a book last updated 13:58:43 ET: at 14:00 the book is 77 seconds old and fails the 60-second gate even with a causal availability witness. Conversely, an actually fresh book received after the cutoff remains unavailable at that mark. These fixtures supply synthetic authoritative book and availability clocks separately; they are not a decoder or evidence that such witnesses exist in retained data.

Databento's [OPRA pre-March 28, 2023 notes](https://databento.com/docs/datasets/opra-pillar#data-prior-to-2023-03-28) say that period was sourced from subsampled data, not original packet captures. Databento documents `F_BAD_TS_RECV` always set and no `cmbp-1`, `cbbo-1s` or `tcbbo` for that period; `cbbo-1m` is its finest BBO there. Its general pre-2023 receive-clock caveat does not override the specific CBBO interval-field semantics or establish a durable as-of receipt. The entire former D0 H1 window (2018-01-02 through 2022-11-11) is before that boundary. No G5 acquisition or H1 qualification is planned under revision 7. For any separate authorized use, do not copy a bucket-end or trade clock into a quote clock; require the applicable provenance and availability evidence.
