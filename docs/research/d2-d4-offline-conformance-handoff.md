## Runnable D2 conformance slice
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_spy_vol_quote_diagnostics.py -v`

The diagnostic consumes explicitly provided canonical updates; no vendor/path access, fetch, registry, scoring or attempt to qualify live data. It is intentionally not invoked by any existing running service. The quote-mark gate accepts a single generation, uses the newest causal update for each supplied fixed leg, and refuses invalid or conflicting latest states rather than borrowing older quotes. It applies the signed 60-second/maximum-spread rule and tests DST instant comparison. A successful check reports FOUR_QUOTES_QUALIFIED and NOT_ESTABLISHED for execution; it cannot label MODELED_BBO_SAME_MARK without separately bound matching spot. ATM selection, spot/deadline policy, actual eligibility, contracts, fills and payoff are separate. Before any use beyond fixtures, the caller must freeze the immutable, complete input generation and mark, and authenticate and qualify update clocks, identities and sources. Differing prices at the same observation are rejected even with different arrival times because arrival is not an authoritative revision sequence; identical repeats retain the earliest eligible availability. Malformed/nonfinite or unsupported Decimal scales and magnitudes fail closed rather than permitting ambient arithmetic to alter the result. The diagnostic admits at most 18 Decimal coefficient digits, exponents −12 through 6, and an upper price bound of $1 million (input safety, not a strategy filter).
