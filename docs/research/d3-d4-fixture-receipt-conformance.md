No extra registration, pilot, chain or readiness signal. Rehearse the decision/nontrade/unavailable denominator using a pre-frozen list of expected IDs and per-opportunity cutoff. The custody owner/reviewer must bind the actual roster and per-opportunity deadlines to the manifest and validate authoritative post-commit evidence and exact ID/hash; none of those is supplied here. Deadline must precede outcome access; no outcome clock is read by the fixture helper.
