# SPY red-band crossings v1

Registration date: November 12, 2026, after the cash close. First eligible session: November 13, 2026. November 12 supplied the motivating example. Live results begin with zero observations. The recorder uses the private GRID SPY same-day-expiry strike journal and completed Schwab SPY one-minute bars. Each red strike can produce one flag of each kind per session.

**First cross.** The previous close was at or below a negative strike and the current close is strictly above it. The adjacent $1 strike below must be positive. Scan upward along the contiguous $1 ladder to the first positive strike. A missing or zero cell interrupts the scan. Freeze both green strikes using only the GEX snapshot recorded in the minute before this price bar. The upside target is the upper green. If price has already closed at or beyond that target, the crossing is consumed without a flag.

**Failed reclaim.** After the first cross, price must close below the red, later close back above, then close below again before the reclaim reaches the frozen upper green. Confirm the band still has the same green boundaries. A lower-green touch during the loss before the reclaim ends that setup. An upper-green touch during the reclaim defeats that attempt. A decline whose confirmation close is already at or below the lower target cannot issue a downward flag. The downside target is the lower green. Missing or nonconsecutive inputs interrupt the sequence.

**Time and outcome.** Save the exchange session date, confirmation time (the signal bar's close), actual issue time, frozen strikes, confirming price, deadline and outcome minute. Source clocks are UTC; the board displays Eastern time with daylight saving. The confirmation time assigns the bucket: opening 09:30–09:59, morning 10:00–11:29, midday 11:30–13:29, afternoon 13:30–15:29, closing 15:30–16:00. Early closes end recording at the exchange close. Each metric is reported for each setup and time bucket separately.

Only a signal issued while its just-completed bar is current can enter the ledger. Start grading with the first full minute beginning after actual issuance. Score the next 30 such minutes, capped at the cash close. A qualifying high at or above the upside target or low at or below the downside target is a hit. The hit time is the end of the first observed qualifying minute. A miss requires complete bar coverage to the deadline; irrecoverable missing coverage or zero remaining full minutes produces a censored outcome. The hit rate is hits divided by hits plus misses, with pending and censored totals shown alongside. Related events from the same swing can appear in both families. These paper measurements track underlying price objectives, with no assumed fills or profit.

The worker initializes the journal even when the market is closed and runs independently of the dashboard. Keep the existing deployment's private market-data settings. Install deploy/systemd/grid-spy-band-flags.service.template as grid-spy-band-flags.service and enable it. Its versioned append-only journal resides alongside the GRID time maps. The authenticated read endpoint is /api/v1/gamma-watch/spy-band-flags. The date picker filters the event list; scorecard totals include all prospective sessions.
